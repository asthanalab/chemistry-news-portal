Chemistry news portal

Privacy

Updated October 27, 2026.

Departmental news

Submitted news, attachments and office notes are private to the department office. Contributors do not need a Google account. A browser cookie lets contributors return to their own unfinished drafts. The portal stores submissions privately on Cloudflare; source code on GitHub does not contain submissions or credentials.

Administrator recovery with Google

Google sign-in is used only to verify the approved administrator when recovering the office password. It requests the Google account identity and email address. It does not request permission to read or send Gmail, access contacts or calendars, or read Google Drive. The portal verifies the signed identity response and stores the approved account's unique identifier and email address to prevent another account from taking over recovery. Google account access and refresh tokens are not retained. No news or attachments are sent to Google during this sign-in.

Recovery uses temporary browser cookies and short-lived, single-use credentials. Successful recovery lets the administrator choose a new office password; it does not automatically open the inbox. Changing the password signs out existing office sessions. Requests are rate limited using a keyed hash of the network address. The portal does not use advertising or analytics tracking.

Private backups

The separately authorized Drive backup script stores private copies of news and images in the administrator's Google Drive folder. That script has access to its own files, not the account's inbox. Recovery sign-in does not grant backup access. Backups and news are never emailed.

Control and retention

The office can delete individual live news items. Existing backups remain until their owner deletes them. Verified backups may precede automatic cleanup at the configured storage limit. The hosting administrator controls recovery configuration and can remove or replace the stored administrator identity. Expired recovery credentials cannot be used and are cleared during subsequent recovery activity or a password change.
